opitlcalOS — Learning Edition

opitlcalOS is an original, fictional educational simulation. It is not an actual Linux distribution and is not affiliated with Fedora, GNOME, IBM, Henry Heffernan, or Geometry Dash.

The workstation models and baked textures in /models/henry/ are reused from:
https://github.com/henryjeff/portfolio-website
Commit: c53c5a50183655eb83d70048403a5084f5d1214c
License: MIT (full notice below and at /models/henry/LICENSE.txt).
The camera choreography, CSS3D screen approach, monochrome outer UI, and
coffee steam shader are adapted from the same MIT-licensed source.
Film grain and CRT noise are procedural; no source videos are bundled.

Credits embedded in the original texture atlas:
- Henry Heffernan — Texturing + UV
- Mickael Boitte — Computer Model
- Sean Nicolas — Environment Models

Included assets: computer_setup.glb, environment.glb, decor.glb,
baked_computer.jpg, baked_environment.jpg, baked_decor_modified.jpg,
smudges.jpg, shadow-compressed.png. Asset files are copied unchanged; the
portfolio paper mesh uses a blank material in the scene. Original hardware branding
remains part of the model texture.

opitlcalOS's desktop, wallpaper, lesson geometry, and synthesized audio are
original to this project. The source portfolio's OS, personal content,
music, and video are not shown. No Geometry Dash level art or music
is distributed.
